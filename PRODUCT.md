# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents, new families, school leadership, staff, and community partners who need to understand École Champlain quickly, trust the school, and find practical information without hunting through dense navigation.

## Product Purpose

Modernize the public website into a persuasive, interactive prototype that preserves the school's existing information while making the first impression feel confident, warm, premium, and easier to approve.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, capable, and proud. The site should feel like a guided learning voyage: rooted in Acadie and the Champlain identity, but polished enough for a leadership presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic school templates, childish clipart, fake performance metrics, repetitive card grids, stale CMS chrome, low-contrast gray text, and decorative animation that gets in the way of parent tasks.

## Design Principles

- Lead with identity: the Champlain logo story, Acadie colors, and maritime learning journey should be visible immediately.
- Make parent tasks obvious: registration, transportation, calendar, contact, delays, and district links must be one click away.
- Preserve content, improve confidence: keep the existing school information intact while cleaning obvious typos and stale filler.
- Make depth feel organized: long school pages need side navigation, search, related links, and readable sections.
- Motion should guide: animated waves, hover states, and menu transitions should help the user understand where they are.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly navigation, reduced-motion alternatives, and readable French typography across desktop and mobile.
